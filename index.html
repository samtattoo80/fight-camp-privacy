Fight Camp: Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Fight Camp is an iPhone app for tracking a boxer's weight, food, drink and training through a fight camp. It is made by Sandr Audio. This page says what the app does with your information. The short version: almost everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sold, shared or used to track you.

What Fight Camp keeps, and where

Everything you enter is kept only on your phone:

  • your profile (name, and optionally date of birth and height)
  • your camp: fight date, weigh-in date, weight class
  • your weigh-ins
  • the food and drink you log, your daily targets, and foods you save
  • your camp calendar entries
  • your settings, such as units and timer choices

There is no account, no sign-in and no server that holds a copy. We (Sandr Audio) cannot see any of it, and cannot get it back for you if you delete the app.

What leaves your phone

Only one thing: when you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search words or the barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts, a free, open food database, to look the food up. The request also carries what any internet request carries, such as your IP address, and the app's name (“RopesFightCamp”).

It does not send your name, your weight, your profile, your logs, your calendar, or any device or advertising identifier, and it does not use cookies. Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy at world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

If you never search for a food or scan a barcode, nothing leaves your phone.

The camera

Fight Camp asks for the camera only when you tap “Scan a barcode”. The picture is read on your phone to find the barcode number. It is not saved and is not sent anywhere; only the number is looked up, as above.

No tracking, no ads

Fight Camp has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects information about you. Nothing about you is sold or shared with anyone.

Apple and TestFlight

If you installed Fight Camp through Apple's TestFlight, Apple handles the install and may pass crash reports, and any feedback you choose to send, to the developer, under Apple's own privacy policy and your phone's analytics settings. Your phone's own backups (for example, iCloud Backup, if you have it on) may include the app's data; that is controlled by Apple and your phone settings, not by Fight Camp.

Deleting your information

You can delete a single weigh-in, food entry or calendar entry inside the app. Deleting the app from your phone removes all of its data from the phone.

Not medical advice

Fight Camp tracks and plans. It is not medical advice and it cannot see your health. Losing weight fast, and cutting water in particular, can hurt you. Talk to your coach or a doctor before you change how you eat or drink to make weight, and stop if you feel unwell.

Children

Fight Camp is not aimed at children. Because the app collects nothing about anyone, it holds no information about children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
